# Scan Trap

Use `$trap-detector` when the user mentions:

- friend recommendation.
- group/teacher/insider information.
- guaranteed profit, must rise, double soon.
- recommendations from 小红书、抖音、B站、知乎、公众号 or paid communities.

Output:

- risk level.
- each signal: hit / not hit / insufficient data.
- concrete evidence sources when available.
- recommendation capped at risk warning, not a buy/sell instruction.
