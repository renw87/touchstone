# Quick Scan

Use `$a-share-investment-research` for a lightweight first pass before full analysis.

Check:

- ST/suspension/delisting/liquidity gates.
- latest announcements or earnings risk.
- basic trend and support/resistance.
- obvious theme/business mismatch.
- whether a full Harness run is warranted.

Output:

- `no_trade` or `watch` only.
- reasons and missing data.
- next action: full research, trap scan, LHB scan, or discard.
