# Investment Committee

Use this panel to debate an A-share thesis before any trade-level signal.

## Seats

- Value quality: cash flow, ROE, moat, margin of safety.
- Growth: TAM, revenue growth, product cycle, operating leverage.
- Macro and policy: liquidity, sector policy, regulation, rate/exchange-rate pressure.
- Technical: trend, momentum, support/resistance, volatility.
- China market microstructure: ST, suspension, price limits, unlocks, pledges, LHB, financing.
- Quant and backtest: rule clarity, sample quality, drawdown, failure cases.
- Bear case: strongest reason the thesis is wrong.

## Output

- strongest long argument.
- strongest short/bear argument.
- key evidence still missing.
- signal boundary: `no_trade`, `watch`, or allowed to evaluate trade signal.
