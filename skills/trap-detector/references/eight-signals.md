# Eight Trap Signals

| ID | Signal | Typical Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low-quality account swarm | many similar posts, low interaction, same wording |
| 2 | Template language | "主力建仓完毕", "目标翻倍", "即将爆发" |
| 3 | Paid funnel | WeChat group, VIP class, livestream room, teacher invite |
| 4 | Fundamentals mismatch | weak financials but sudden heavy promotion |
| 5 | Price pre-pump | large rise before recommendation wave |
| 6 | Teacher persona | "老师带单", "股神", "内幕圈" |
| 7 | Cross-platform push | simultaneous push across 小红书/抖音/B站/公众号 |
| 8 | Fake catalyst | unverifiable report, rumor, policy distortion |

Risk levels:

- 0-1 hit: green.
- 2-3 hits: yellow.
- 4-5 hits: orange.
- 6+ hits: red.

If the user mentions "稳赚", "内幕", "必涨", "翻倍", increase severity by at least one level.
